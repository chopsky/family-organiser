Back to school

The school year, sorted before it starts

Term starts in a couple of weeks — and with it the newsletters, the kit lists, the club schedules and the “is it an INSET day?” texts.
Ten minutes in Housemait now and September runs itself. Here’s the checklist.

The ten-minute checklist

01 School dates  ·  02 Newsletters  ·  03 WhatsApp  ·  04 Routines

01

Two minutes

Put the term dates on the family calendar

Add your school and this year’s term dates, half terms and holidays land on the calendar everyone in the house can see — and stay updated when the school adds INSET days later. Activity reminders pause themselves in the holidays too.

Where: More → School → Add a school  ·  or open it on the web

02

The magic trick

Forward the first newsletter — we’ll read it

When the first school newsletter lands in September, don’t read it with a highlighter — forward it to your Housemait address. Every date inside — the bake sale, the trip, the mufti day — goes straight onto the family calendar, assigned to the right child.

Where: Settings → Connected services → Send Emails to AI — copy your address, save it as a contact

03

Book-bag paperwork

Snap it on WhatsApp, it’s in the diary

Party invites, fixture lists, club timetables — the paper that comes home crumpled at the bottom of the book bag. Take a photo, send it to Housemait on WhatsApp, and it’s on the calendar before the kettle’s boiled. Voice notes and plain texts work too.

Where: Settings → Connected services → Connect WhatsApp

04

Before the first school run

Set the school-morning routine now

Teeth, uniform, book bag, shoes — build the morning routine while the house is still calm, and let the kids tick it off themselves for stars from day one. A routine that starts on the first morning sticks; one that starts in week three doesn’t.

Where: Tasks → Routines → Add routine — kids tick off in Child Mode
